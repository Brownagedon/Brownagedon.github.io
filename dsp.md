## Digital signal processing for acoustic instruments

Current research exploring how embedded digital signal processing can become part of an acoustic instrument's natural vocabulary. Focusing on control interfaces that are intuitive for musicians instead of feeling like a separate technology layer.

### Areas of exploration

- Instrument-integrated electronics
- Experimental signal processing
- Composing for electroacoustics within the affordances and constraints of jazz

This research was awarded grant support in September 2025.
