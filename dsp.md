## Digital signal processing for acoustic instruments

Grey's current research explores how embedded digital signal processing can become part of an acoustic instrument's natural vocabulary. The work focuses on control interfaces that are intuitive for musicians instead of feeling like a separate technology layer.

### Areas of exploration

- Instrument-integrated electronics
- Analog harmonization
- Experimental signal processing
- TinyML and responsive systems

This research was awarded grant support in September 2025.
